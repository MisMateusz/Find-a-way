Privacy notice

  • With a direct connection, the other player can see your IP address.
  • Servers involved: PeerJS (sees your IP and the lobby code), Supabase (public leaderboard with nicknames and times, and the secure connection) and GitHub Pages (hosting).
  • The secure connection sends game messages through the Supabase server, so the other player does not see your IP address. However, this way has set maximum games per month.
  • Do not use your real name as a nickname.
  • Do not share your code to random players.
